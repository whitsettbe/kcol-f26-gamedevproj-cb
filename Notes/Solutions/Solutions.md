**Level Name:** Out of the Corner  
**Authors:** Benjamin  
**Intended layer:** Caves  

1. Roy: in the right half of the first room, push the left unentangled block up, then the middle one to the right, then move up.
2. Leonard: push the unentangled block left two squares and up two squares.
3. Roy: push the adjacent unentangled and entangled blocks left once.
4. Leonard: push the unentangled block up into the hole in the hallway and enter the hallway.
5. Roy: push left three times (the entangled block will fall into the hole) and enter the hallway.
6. Leonard: push the entangled block in the hallway down to fill the hole in Roy's hallway.
7. Roy: walk to the exit.
8. Leonard: walk to the exit.

**Level Name:** Jump Around  
**Authors:** Benjamin  
**Intended layer:** Ruins  

1. Leonard: walk up onto the portal.
2. Roy: walk left onto the portal.
3. Leonard: walk onto the portal at bottom-left of the central room.
4. Roy: walk onto the portal at top-left of the central room.
5. Leonard: push your block up, rightward to the wall, up, up. This will teleport you back to the central room.
6. Roy: push your block rightward twice. This will teleport you back to the central room.
7. Leonard: walk onto the portal at top-right of the central room.
8. Roy: use the top-right portal in the central room.
9. Leonard: push the block in the hallway rightward into the hole.
10. Roy: push your block leftward twice, thereby returning to the central room.
11. Leonard: walk to the exit.
12. Roy: walk to the exit.
